FRAMECUT is a video editor portfolio built with HTML5, Tailwind CSS, and custom animations. Its visual identity pairs a dark background with vivid lime accents, bold condensed typography, and cinematic imagery.

The hero introduces the editor with a featured showreel concept, creative statement, and project highlights. The selected work gallery presents four projects: Into The Wild, The Experience, New Perspective, and After The Lights.

The services section covers video editing, color grading, motion design, and sound design. A four-stage process explains discovery, production editing, refinement, and final delivery. The about section introduces Alex Morgan and his approach to visual storytelling.

A contact section invites film, campaign, and creative project enquiries. The footer includes navigation, social links, studio details, and an email address.

Built with Tailwind CSS, responsive grids, image zoom effects, hover overlays, reveal animations, animated accents, and reduced-motion support, FRAMECUT presents a vivid portfolio focused on cinematic editing and visual storytelling.
